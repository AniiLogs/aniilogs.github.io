AniiLogs Companion

A free desktop app that sits next to Aniimo: a boss DPS meter in the game's own style, your fight results and Battle Data kept automatically, and your records on the AniiLogs leaderboards if you want them there.

Download for Windows Windows 10 or 11 · about 50 MB · all versions

Boss DPS meter

Team DPS, the last five seconds and team damage while you fight, with the boss's HP left right on its bar. It also tracks the BREAK gauge: break points left, how fast your team drains it, and how many breaks you landed.

Results kept for you

Every Holo-Battle result screen and Battle Data screen is saved with its numbers. The app can open View Battle Data for you, so button-mashing past the result screen doesn't lose it.

Leaderboards

Opt in and your Interlink scores, stars and clear times go to the AniiLogs leaderboards under your in-game name: each week's bosses, the week total and the season. AI teammates are marked.

Sanctum Helper included

The Egg Heist companion by sargeeva: the whole Lost Sanctum layout on your map, egg nests, key rooms and where you are. Its options have their own page in the app.

Getting started

  1. Run the installer. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't code-signed yet: choose More info, then Run anyway. It installs for your Windows user only.
  2. Play Aniimo in Borderless or Windowed mode. The meter appears with a boss's HP bar and hides again after the fight.
  3. Optional: connect your account. Sign in on AniiLogs, open Settings › Desktop app, create a token and paste it into the app under DPS Meter › Leaderboards & account.

Fair play

The app only looks at screen pixels, using Windows' own window capture (no yellow capture border), and reads the game's own text log. It never reads or changes the game's memory, never injects anything and never modifies the game's files. It reads the game's HUD font and weekly boss schedule from your own game install.
