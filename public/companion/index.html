aLogs

Aniiloger's Companion App: a free desktop app that runs next to Aniimo, with no ads, ever. A boss DPS meter in the game's own style, an Egg Heist overlay, your fight results and Battle Data saved for you, and, if you want, your records on the AniiLogs leaderboards and your AniiLogs profile kept up to date as you play.

Download for Windows Windows 10 or 11 · about 50 MB · all versions · it updates itself after that

Ad-free boss DPS meter

Team DPS, the last five seconds and team damage while you fight, with the boss's HP left right on its bar. It also tracks the BREAK gauge: break points left, how fast your team drains it, and how many breaks you landed. Out in the world, the boss's kill sound marks the exact end of the fight.

Results kept for you

Every Holo-Battle result screen and Battle Data screen is saved with its numbers. The app can open View Battle Data for you, so button-mashing past the result screen doesn't lose it.

Leaderboards

Opt in and your records go to the AniiLogs leaderboards under your in-game name: Holo-Battle Interlink scores and stars (each week's bosses, the week total and the season), Holo-Battle Sim clear times, and your Egg Heist season rank and matches. AI teammates are marked.

Your AniiLogs profile

Your character's name, level, gender and server; your Collection Progress from the game's Profile screen; your Egg Heist rank with its badge; in-game photos you choose to upload; and Online or AFK while you play. Your profile is private until you make it public.

Egg Heist overlay

Sanctum Helper, the Egg Heist overlay by sargeeva: the whole Lost Sanctum layout on your map, egg nests, key rooms and where you are. Its options have their own page in the app.

Keeps itself up to date

aLogs checks for new versions and installs them when you choose. Something wrong? Report a problem on its Dashboard sends your logs (and screenshots, if you add some) to the developers.

Getting started

  1. Run the installer. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't code-signed yet: choose More info, then Run anyway. It installs for your Windows user only.
  2. Follow the setup guide. When aLogs first opens, a few short steps sign you in with Discord (your browser opens; approve it there) and let you choose what goes to the leaderboards. You can skip it and sign in later with Sign in to sync in the sidebar.
  3. Play Aniimo in Borderless or Windowed mode. The meter appears with a boss's HP bar and hides again after the fight.
  4. Link your character. Open the game menu (Esc) or your Profile (Esc › Profile) once while aLogs is running: it reads your name with your UID, which proves the character is yours. Opening your Profile also sends its Collection Progress. Renamed? Use Link character on the Dashboard.
  5. Optional: photos. Set the game's Settings › Storage to Local (or Local and cloud), then pick photos on the app's Photos page to put on your profile.

Signed in, your theme and "Show my online status" are kept with your AniiLogs account: change them in the app or on the site and both follow.

Fair play

The app looks at screen pixels, using Windows' own window capture (no yellow capture border), and reads the game's own text log. During a boss fight it listens to Aniimo's audio only (nothing else on your PC) for the kill sound, and records nothing. It never reads or changes the game's memory, never injects anything and never modifies the game's files. From your own game install it reads the HUD font, the weekly boss schedule and your saved photos, and from the game's settings the server you play on.
