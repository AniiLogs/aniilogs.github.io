Site update: Whisperwake Isles (game build 3846247) October 29, 2026
  • Whisperwake Isles across the site: a new map, 42 new Aniimo forms, duels, Alphas and Omega Jeburst.
  • Collection: 9 new Season 1 Keepsakes.
  • Aniilog: sorted by region, showcase videos for every Aniimo, Resonance materials for every form, and 77 new research guides.
  • Team Builder: every new form with all its skills traced, the Aniimo picker grouped by region, and upgrades that show their conditions.
  • Item-log: about 635 new items, a "New in game build" filter, many new sources, and where items are used for breakthrough and evolution.
  • Map: 2 new Elite Pathfinders on Astra, boss reward pins and new container icons.
  • Checklist: now its own page, with the new Aniimo, more Lumin rows and a new Bosses tab. Your progress carries over.
  • Fixes: broken and wrong trait icons, buff carry-over info, raw text in skills, misplaced markers, test items removed from the Item-log, and outdated item descriptions and rarities.
