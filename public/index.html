AniiLogs

AniiLogs is an unofficial, fan-made site for Aniimo. It puts the game's data and players' records in one place.

  • Interactive Map Every region's points of interest, resources, bosses and respawn timers.
  • Aniilog Every Aniimo form: stats, skills, traits, evolutions and where to find it.
  • Item-log The game's items and where they come from.
  • Checklists Track what you've caught and collected.
  • Team Builder Plan a team, compare stats and work out damage.
  • Leaderboards Holo-Battle Interlink, Holo-Battle Sim and Egg Heist records, read from the game's result screens.
  • aLogs The Windows companion app: a boss DPS meter and Egg Heist overlay that can send your records here.

Game update: November 19 patch (build 3989635) November 19, 2026

In the game

  • What this covers: the November 19 patch. The game has posted no notes for it, so this is what its files show.
  • Operation: Egg Heist: the rank reward and the shop listing announced on November 17 are now in the files. Egg Emperor with 200 stars gives a Departure with the Wind: Collectible Gift Box (choose one of the season's 11 collectibles), and the Egg Heist Shop lists the Departure with the Wind: Collectible Blind Box for 500,000 Eggshell Coin, one a week, from Egg Emperor.
  • Nighttime Gathering: 20 new item delivery quests (Weird Jelly Beans, Breezy Plains and Whisperwake Isles Dewdrop Crystals, Aniipod, Aniipod Pro and Aniipod Mega), and 4 large ore quests that could never come up now can.
  • Events: the Whisperwake Isles Exploration Plan stays in the event list until November 24 (it was to leave after November 19).
  • Text: 1,282 English strings reworded. A group of Pathfinders is now a Team (Create Team, Leave Team, Team Channel) and the Aniimo you bring are your Party (Aniimo Party, party members). 23 items have a new name.
  • Looks: outfit data for the Shadow Rose set.
  • Unchanged: every Aniimo, skill and buff value, the damage formulas, Holo-Battle Interlink, the Sanctum, and the map's spawns and chests. Frenzied Assault (Saws, Whirlbite) still gives 0%.

On the site

  • Item-log: the Collectible Gift Box added with its 11 choices (the "New in game build" filter shows it), the Collectible Blind Box's shop price, 21 renamed items and new text on 134 items.
  • Aniilog and Team Builder: the reworded skill, trait and buff texts, on 25 forms of 12 Aniimo.
  • Last note: one correction. It said 18 Nighttime Gathering quests got an order type; that was our misread of the table, and those rows did not change.
  • Guides: the Damage Guide, duels, type matchups and the Interlink guide re-checked against this build: no changes needed.
  • Map and Checklists: nothing to change.

Game update: November 12–18 fixes (build 3973445) November 18, 2026

In the game

  • What this covers: the official fix lists of November 12, 13, 17 and 18, checked against the game's files, plus what the files show that the lists don't.
  • Operation: Egg Heist: a new rank reward at Egg Emperor with 200 stars (a Collectible Gift Box: pick one Departure with the Wind collectible), the shop sells the Collectible Blind Box to Egg Emperor players with a weekly limit, and the Giga-Egg jumps less and is knocked back a little when hit. In Fair Arena, the Lightning Needle held item no longer breaks other Pathfinders' armor when it shouldn't.
  • Aniimo Dash: Mass Slow now tells the racers it hits, the Smoke Bomb displays correctly (its smoke lasts 2 s instead of 3 s), the Group Race count of completed races is fixed, and so are the Thundercloud and Magmarex problems.
  • Nighttime Gathering: item delivery quests refresh every 4 hours instead of every 6, the Premium Gemstone Dust quest can be turned in, refreshing no longer takes Home Coins without refreshing, and Season Orders no longer disappear.
  • Fixes: Lunara's missing skill effects, Prismana Hypnyto's model, the right stick on the map with a controller, the chat tab, an Ultimate's shown cooldown running longer than the real one, removing members from a Private RV Park, Aniimo Discovery losing an event Aniimo, the Voxel Coin cap, missing RV Park furniture and the Huffy accessory that couldn't be claimed.
  • Events: Jaguaraun's Resonance Fantasia is postponed. It was due on November 20 and now runs from after the November 26 update to December 17, 03:59 server time; items to help raise Jaguaraun will come by in-game mail as an apology. This build takes the event, and Thundercharge Cluster's listing in the Item Exchange, out of the files until then.
  • Not in the official notes: the An Aniimo Is Born Co-Dev Contest page, which was in the last build's files, is gone. Coral Sea of Dreams and Squarrel's Poem of Revolution now give an Archival Chip each. Advanced Gemstone Dust is now Premium Gemstone Dust.
  • Still open, from the official list: Holo-Battle Interlink results can be tallied just before a challenge ends, and its countdown can jump back at the start. In Aniimo Dash, failing to turn into Thundercloud can make your Party disappear.
  • Unchanged: every Aniimo, skill and buff value, the main damage formulas, Holo-Battle Interlink, the Sanctum and the map. Frenzied Assault (Saws, Whirlbite) still gives 0%.

On the site

  • Item-log: Premium Gemstone Dust renamed, the two Archival Chips added with their quests (the "New in game build" filter shows them), Thundercharge Cluster no longer lists a shop, and the makeup texts.
  • Last note: reworded with the game's own names (Aniimo Dash, Aniimo Affinity, Home Season, Operation: Egg Heist and more), and Resonance Fantasia's new dates are marked there.
  • Guides: the Damage Guide, duels, type matchups and the Interlink guide re-checked against this build: no changes needed.
  • Aniilog, Team Builder, Map and Checklists: nothing to change.

Game update: Aniimo Dash (build 3934365) November 12, 2026

In the game

  • Aniimo Dash: a racing event on Breezy Plains with 3 tracks (Greenfield Shallows now, Gullcove Cliffs on Nov 19, Redrock Trail on Nov 26), a solo and a group mode, 14 track items, Racing Coin and a Racing Shop.
  • Racing items: the Mass Slow item slows by 50% instead of 20% but only for 3 s instead of 10 s, and the race Stun lasts 1.5 s instead of 2 s. Only racing uses this Stun; Aniimo skill stuns are unchanged.
  • Aniimo skills: Crocobask's Deepwater Croc King hits 5 times instead of 3, Dewlop and Dewple's Rapids Dewdrop drains 10 Energy a second instead of 100 (as its text says; both need verification), and Hexxin's Curse and Black Hole Domain are reworked.
  • Skill texts: 18 texts on 69 Aniimo forms reworded, wording only (for example "Max HP" is now "max HP").
  • Coraliz: Rock Element Level 1 → 3, Water 1 → 2, and higher Home Abilities levels. Whirlbite gets a Limited-Time Whirlbite Reset until Nov 27.
  • Aniimo Affinity (new): 4 Affinity Levels (100, 500 and 1,200 Affinity for levels 2, 3 and 4), with daily and weekly limits for each way of earning it.
  • Operation: Egg Heist: two random maps leave the rotation, Team Mode opponents are capped at level 55 (60 on difficulty 5), and Egg Heist Alphas' Enhance stacks to 7 instead of 5.
  • Items: 27 new, 15 renamed (Bud Ticket is now Wishstar, Fallsplash Grass is now Cascadewort), 14 with new sources and 3 with a new rarity.
  • Events: Prismana Snapshots (photograph Prismana Royalby, then Jeburst, then Glameep, until Nov 29), and Jaguaraun's Fantasia Survey from Nov 19 (since postponed to after the Nov 26 update).
  • Quests: two new chains, Coral Sea of Dreams (Crescent Bay) and Squarrel's Poem of Revolution (Mirrorsea Village).
  • Homeland: a new Home Storage for Aniimo you aren't using (separate from the Aniimo living in your Home), shareable plot layouts (Plot Code), rewards for completed furniture sets, cheaper pavements, and Park-wide totals for the season's Park Co-building.
  • Looks and shop: Face Presets and Makeup Presets, outfit sets, Try On outfits and a freer Photo Studio; new outfits Valiant Knight (Nov 13) and Shadow Rose (Nov 20).
  • Settings: 7 report reasons, main UI side padding, a Thai Keyboard button layout and PlayStation/Microsoft unlinking.
  • Also: a Home Season with 16 new Season Buffs, Prismana Whirlbite at Cloudreach Falls instead of Jeburst, 3 new Flameruff on Breezy Plains, and the Holo-Battle Interlink buff Coordinated Strike is now Coordinated Offensive.
  • Unchanged: damage formulas, the element chart, Holo-Battle Interlink bosses and weeks. Frenzied Assault (Saws, Whirlbite) still gives 0%.

On the site

  • Team Builder: texts updated from the game, Deepwater Croc King counts 5 hits, and Selene's Judgment lasts until combat ends.
  • Aniilog: 221 updates, including Coraliz's new levels.
  • Item-log: the 27 new items, renames, sources and rarities, and the "New in game build" filter now shows this update's items.
  • Map: the new Flameruff, the Whisperwake marker fixes and Cascadewort; every chest and your Opened ticks are kept.
  • Guides: Coordinated Offensive in the Interlink guide, and the Damage Guide re-checked against this build.

Site update: Whisperwake Isles (game build 3846247) October 29, 2026
  • Whisperwake Isles across the site: a new map, 42 new Aniimo forms, duels, Alphas and Omega Jeburst.
  • Collection: 9 new Season 1 Keepsakes.
  • Aniilog: sorted by region, showcase videos for every Aniimo, Resonance materials for every form, and 77 new research guides.
  • Team Builder: every new form with all its skills traced, the Aniimo picker grouped by region, and upgrades that show their conditions.
  • Item-log: about 635 new items, a "New in game build" filter, many new sources, and where items are used for breakthrough and evolution.
  • Map: 2 new Elite Pathfinders on Astra, boss reward pins and new container icons.
  • Checklist: now its own page, with the new Aniimo, more Lumin rows and a new Bosses tab. Your progress carries over.
  • Fixes: broken and wrong trait icons, buff carry-over info, raw text in skills, misplaced markers, test items removed from the Item-log, and outdated item descriptions and rarities.
