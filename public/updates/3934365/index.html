Game update: World Racing

Game build 3934365November 12, 2026

World RacingSkillsSkill textsAniimoBondHome seasonHomelandEventsQuestsEgg HeistWorldItemsLooksShopSettingsTextOn the site

At a glance

  • New: World Racing on Breezy Plains, with 3 tracks opening one a week, a solo and a group mode, 14 track items, Racing Coin and a Racing Shop.
  • Aniimo skills: Crocobask's Deepwater Croc King hits more times, Dewlop and Dewple's Rapids Dewdrop drains 10 Energy a second as its text says (it drained 100), and Hexxin's Curse and Black Hole Domain are reworked. 18 skill texts on 69 forms are reworded, with no number changes.
  • Coraliz has higher element and homeland levels, and Whirlbite gets a free balance reset until November 27.
  • Bond now tops out at level 4, with new amounts per level and separate daily caps for each way of earning it.
  • Home season: 16 new home buffs, daily buffs and Pumpkin Aeon construction stages.
  • Egg Heist: two random maps leave the rotation, Team Mode opponents get level caps, and Egg Heist Alphas' Enhance stacks to 7.
  • Events: Prismana Snapshots (photograph Prismana Royalby, Jeburst, then Glameep, until November 29) and, from November 19, Jaguaraun’s Fantasia Survey.
  • Two new quest chains: Coral Sea of Dreams and Squarrel’s Poem of Revolution.
  • Homeland: a new Home Storage for Aniimo you aren’t using, shareable plot layouts, rewards for completed furniture sets, cheaper pavements, and Park totals for the season’s construction.
  • Looks: face and makeup presets, outfit sets, trial outfits and a freer Photo Studio. New outfits Valiant Knight (November 13) and Shadow Rose (November 20).
  • Settings: report reasons, screen-edge padding, a Thai keyboard layout and account unlinking.
  • Items: 27 new items, 15 renamed (Bud Ticket is now Wishstar) and 167 names tidied, 14 with new sources and 3 rarity changes.
  • Still the same: damage formulas, the element chart, Interlink bosses and weeks. Frenzied Assault still gives 0%.

In the game

From the game’s own files (compared with build 3857679). Where the game’s text and its data disagree, we go by the data.

New: World Racing

A racing event on Breezy Plains (the “Breezy Plains Cup”). You race as your Aniimo: combat skills are off, so only mobility skills count.

  • Full Throttle (solo): open all day; you race for your best time and grade.
  • Speed Showdown (group): up to 8 players, every day 18:00–24:00, with Random Item Boxes on the track.
  • Racing parties are separate: 4 Aniimo, 3 active. Your open-world party doesn’t change. You can’t untwine during a race.
  • Everyone has the same Stamina, and mobility skills recharge faster, in races only.
  • Before each race you pick one buff: High-Speed Dash (faster Hustle), Wavecrash Swim (faster swimming and surfing) or Galesprint Wings (faster flight). It stays on when you switch Aniimo.
  • Start-of-race buff: it now cuts skill cooldowns by 80% instead of 50%, and it no longer slows flight sprinting by 20%.
  • On some tracks, twining with certain Aniimo starts a Special Track Event.

Home season (Pumpkin Aeon)

  • 16 new home-season buffs: Aniimo in your Home use no Energy; +100% production at Wells, Tidewhisper Sandcastles, Nimbus Beds, Dewy Houses, Floral Windmills (home vehicle level 18) and Starfall Hammocks (level 12); +30% Power Supply Rate; Farmland formulas take 25% less time; Mine formulas need 25% less work; +100% Wishstar production.
  • Daily buffs (until 4:00 AM): double Bubble Gachapon rewards, Home Orders that don’t use up items, double tilling rewards, cheaper Home Order refreshes, and ripe Magical Crops with a 50% chance of an extra Wonder Sugar Crystal.
  • Construction: 5 shared stages. Your own contribution per stage: 30k, 60k, 90k, 130k and 170k, with 17 reward tiers from 20,000.
  • Park total per stage: besides your own share, the whole Park needs 120k, 240k, 360k and 520k for stages 1–4. Park Contribution is the sum of everyone currently in the Park.
  • Your contribution is your turn-ins at your current Park plus your decorations. Decorations convert Habitability 1:1 (regular furniture) or 1:3 (season furniture), up to 100,000 → 70,000. Turn-in contribution is cleared when you leave a Park; your total isn’t.
  • Turn-in Quests: one at a time. An unfinished one refreshes at 4:00 and still counts toward your limit; refreshing costs Home Coins, more each time. Orders must be handed in from your own Home.
  • Later start: the Nighttime Gathering now starts November 12 06:00 UTC (was November 11 20:00), and the Thank-You Gift stage December 3 06:00 UTC (was December 2 20:00), with a new reward and new daily tasks.
  • New Fannie steps in the RV Park quest (the Strange Mask for the Nighttime Gathering).
  • The event now says “Pumpkin Aeon” instead of “Pumpkin God”.

Homeland

  • New: Home Storage for Aniimo. Your Home now has its own Aniimo storage, where you can keep Aniimo you aren’t using. It’s separate from the Aniimo you place in your Home to live and work there. Switch between it and your regular Aniimo Storage from the Home Aniimo screen. Its code was already in the game; this update unlocked it.
  • Plot layouts: save a layout of your Homebuilding Zone or Production Zone and share it as a Plot Code. Applying one clears the plot first; furniture you don’t have enough of, or that doesn’t fit, is skipped, and facilities on plot borders or overlapping other plots aren’t placed or counted.
  • Return to Home and Return to Park (where your RV is parked) are new shortcuts in the menu.
  • Furniture set rewards: completing a set in the Home Handbook now gives a reward. Nine sets give 20 Glimmer each; one gives Moon Farmer and 50 Glimmer (as the Full Moon Giftbox) and one Nocturnal Whimsy and 50 Glimmer (as the Magical Night Giftbox).
  • Cheaper pavements: 10 District pavements cost 21 → 11 Wishstar, and 6 courtyard floors 23 → 12.
  • Home Coin shop: 8 Magic Pumpkin decorations (20,000 to 300,000 Home Coins) were removed.
  • Rhythmic Loop costs 1 Dream Spark in the Home Shop (limit 1). A second speaker, Rhythm Beat, is in the files with no source yet.
  • Three production formulas changed workload (60 → 75, 75 → 90 and 105 → 90).
  • Aniimo in your Home can now sit in two more vehicle seats.
  • Players without Farmland get a Quick Purchase prompt in the Farmland tutorial.

Character looks and Photo Studio

  • Face presets (face shape plus makeup) and separate makeup presets, with Custom and Set tabs and extra slots to unlock. A warning shows when a preset doesn’t fit your body type.
  • Outfit sets: Equip Set (replaces what you’re wearing) and Preview Set, plus Remove Outer Outfit Pieces.
  • Trial outfits with a countdown; limited-time looks can’t be custom-adjusted.
  • Photo Studio: move, raise, rotate and scale characters and Aniimo (0.1× to 2×), switch or delete characters and stickers, and saved templates. A new switch decides whether a preset shows the look saved in it or keeps yours.
  • The wardrobe names its slots: Top, Bottom, Hands, Socks, Shoes, Cloak, Headwear, Face Accessory, Earrings, Neck, Back, Charm and Badge.
  • Tutorials for Appearance, Snap camera modes, Held Item upgrades, Pathfinding and Nurture can be watched again.

Shop

  • New outfits: Valiant Knight from November 13 and Shadow Rose from November 20, 980 Lumin Crystals each, at the top of the store.
  • Discount coupons: 500 Lumin Crystals off appearance items with a 990 minimum spend, or off Season, Limited and Custom bundles with a 2,000 minimum spend. Nothing gives them out yet.
  • Aniimo Transmog Bundle: Buy Bundle (cheaper when buying several) or Buy Directly.

Settings and social

  • Reporting: 7 reasons: illegal or prohibited content, disrupting gameplay, insults or abuse, scams, inappropriate nickname, cheats or scripts, and throwing or going AFK. Egg Heist has its own Report button.
  • Voice chat: new notices when someone joins or leaves, and a join button.
  • Screen edges: separate side padding for the joystick, skills and the Aniimo queue.
  • Button prompts: a Thai keyboard layout.
  • Accounts: PlayStation and Microsoft accounts can be unlinked (you then sign in with your old email).
  • Power saving while idle: the default went from 3 to 30.
  • The Americas server is now named “Aniimo-Americas”.
