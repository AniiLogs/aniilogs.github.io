Game update: small fixes

Game build 3973445November 18, 2026

EventsWorld RacingHomeland seasonItemsQuestsPathfinder ChallengeLooks and shopDamage formulasControlsTextUnchangedOn the site

At a glance

  • A small update: about 35 of the game’s data tables changed, no Aniimo, skill or buff values among them, and no new items.
  • Events: Jaguaraun’s Fantasia Survey and the An Aniimo Is Born Co-Dev Contest, both listed to start on November 19, are no longer in the game’s files.
  • World Racing: the Smoke Bomb’s smoke is shorter, and a few texts were corrected.
  • Homeland season: construction orders refresh every 4 hours instead of every 6.
  • Items: Advanced Gemstone Dust is now Premium Gemstone Dust.
  • Quests: the two quest chains from the last update now give their Archival Chips.
  • Still the same: the main damage formulas, the element chart, Interlink, the Sanctum, Egg Heist’s tables and the map. Frenzied Assault still gives 0%.

In the game

From the game’s own files (compared with build 3934365). Where the game’s text and its data disagree, we go by the data.

Quests

  • Coral Sea of Dreams (Crescent Bay) now gives Archival Chip - The Smallest Coral Sea near its end.
  • Squarrel’s Poem of Revolution (Mirrorsea Village) now gives Archival Chip - Food Revolution, and one of its steps now switches off an Aniimo spawn point when it starts.
  • The names Chompy and Dora were added as text for that chain.
  • 4 steps in older quest chains changed type, and one line of quest text now says “the Institute’s Idyll exploration handbook”.
  • Readable items (letters, notes, archives) now fill in the same placeholders as dialogue does, such as your character’s name.

Pathfinder Challenge

  • Two scripted events that sat on Little Blanket’s duel now belong to Healing Angel’s.
  • The second stage of the Rookie Island Patroller and Walking Map duels has a different title setting.
  • Opponents, levels and rewards are unchanged.

Looks and shop

  • Huffy (the accessory from First Visit to Whisperwake Isles) now has its accessory data for both your character and your Aniimo.
  • Moving an accessory from one Aniimo or slot to another now checks first whether it can be replaced.
  • 17 hairstyle entries are marked as owned from the start.
  • The 5 Creative Voyage profile frames have new art.
  • The title Paw-some Pals got a display setting, the same one the Creative Voyage frames use.
  • Shop art for the Shadow Rose outfit (November 20) was added, and the Nightborn Allure set is flagged to show among rewards.
  • One shop poster no longer says the shop is open only from 18:00 to 02:00.

Needs verification: which hairstyles are free from the start, and where Paw-some Pals now shows.

Damage formulas

  • The formulas the Damage Guide rests on (3001, 3023, 3512, 3513 and the stat formulas) are the same.
  • Formula 3067 changed. It is a second damage formula with its own “egg” factor: ATK is at least 90% of the role’s ATK, damage is at least 0.05, and PvP damage is ×0.3. The egg factor used to multiply only the scaled damage; it now multiplies the flat part too (Flat Damage minus the target’s Flat Damage Reduction).

Needs verification: what uses formula 3067. The game’s files don’t say; the code that calls it runs on the server.

Controls and interface

  • Map with a controller: while a quest’s details are open, the right stick pans the map instead of scrolling the panel.
  • Button-prompt fading is only applied to panels and widgets that support it.

Text

  • Prismana Snapshots: “Capture” is now “Photograph” in its quests and rules, and the rules say “phase” where they said “event” (rewards are per phase).
  • Indestructible (a Sanctum buff): “1 points of Flat Damage Reduction” is now “1 point(s)”. Its numbers are the same: 1 for every 30 P.DEF and M.DEF.
  • The other rewordings are listed with what they belong to above: racing rules, the Mini Cake, makeup items and Premium Gemstone Dust.
  • 3 new strings (Chompy, Dora and the order-limit message), 19 changed and 16 removed (the two removed events’ texts).

Unchanged

  • Every Aniimo’s stats, elements and skills, and every skill and buff value.
  • Damage formulas, crit, the element chart, the duel rules, BREAK and Star-Up: re-checked against this build.
  • Interlink: the same weeks, bosses and grades. The Sanctum and Egg Heist: the same tables.
  • The map: no spawn, chest or Lumin changes.
  • Frenzied Assault (Saws and Whirlbite) still gives 0% Water Boost instead of 25%.
