Game update: November 12–18 fixes

Game build 3973445 (v1.1.3973445.0)November 18, 2026

Official notesEgg HeistAniimo DashNighttime GatheringEventsNot in the notesEvery file changeStill openUnchangedOn the site

At a glance

  • This page covers everything since the November 12 update: the game’s official fix lists of November 12, 13, 17 and 18, checked against the game’s files (build 3934365 to 3973445), plus what the files show that the lists don’t.
  • Operation: Egg Heist: a new Egg Emperor rank reward, a Collectible Blind Box in the shop, a calmer Giga-Egg and a Lightning Needle fix in Fair Arena.
  • Aniimo Dash: Mass Slow notifies the racers it hits, the Smoke Bomb is fixed, and Group Race counts are right.
  • Nighttime Gathering: item delivery quests refresh every 4 hours instead of every 6, and the Premium Gemstone Dust quest can be turned in.
  • Events: Jaguaraun’s Resonance Fantasia is postponed from November 20 to after the November 26 update (it then runs until December 17).
  • Not in the official notes: the Co-Dev Contest page is gone from the files, two quest chains now give their Archival Chips, and Advanced Gemstone Dust is now Premium Gemstone Dust.
  • Still the same: every Aniimo, skill and buff value, the main damage formulas, the element chart, Holo-Battle Interlink, the Sanctum and the map. Frenzied Assault still gives 0%.

In the game

Sources: the game’s patch notes on Steam (v1.1.3973445.0) the official Known Issues & Fixes post and the news channel of the game’s official Discord, set against the game’s own files. Where the game’s text and its data disagree, we go by the data.

Operation: Egg Heist

  • The Lightning Needle fix, in the files: damage formula 3067 has its own mode factor. The factor used to multiply only the scaled damage; it now multiplies the flat part too (Flat Damage minus the target’s Flat Damage Reduction), which is what a held item like Lightning Needle adds.
  • The rest of formula 3067 is the same: ATK is at least 90% of the role’s ATK, damage is at least 0.05, and PvP damage is ×0.3.
  • The rank reward, the shop’s Blind Box and the Giga-Egg change are official; none of them is in the game’s data tables, so there are no numbers to add.

Needs verification: the weekly limit on the Blind Box, and how much less the Giga-Egg jumps.

Events

Resonance Fantasia (Jaguaraun) is postponed

Resonance FantasiaBeforeNow
Event period (local server time)November 20 04:00 – December 10 03:59After the November 26 update – December 17 03:59
  • Announced on the game’s official Discord on November 15: after final checks, some of the event’s features would work better alongside a version update.
  • As an apology, the game will send items to help you raise Jaguaraun by in-game mail at a later date.

  • Thundercharge Cluster (use it at a Bloom to draw Jaguaraun in) is no longer sold in the Item Exchange for 1 Roar Stone. The item and its formula are still in the files.

Not in the official notes

These come from the game’s files only.

The Co-Dev Contest page

  • An Aniimo Is Born – Co-Dev Contest was in the last build’s files for November 19 20:00 UTC to February 14. Its event, its tab in the event list and its info page are gone. It was only ever in the files; no official post mentions it.

Quests

  • Coral Sea of Dreams now gives Archival Chip - The Smallest Coral Sea near its end.
  • Squarrel’s Poem of Revolution now gives Archival Chip - Food Revolution, and one of its steps switches off a spawn point when it starts.
  • 4 steps in older quest chains changed type, and one line of quest text now says “the Institute’s Idyll exploration handbook”.
  • Readable items (letters, notes, archives) now fill in the same placeholders as dialogue does, such as your character’s name.
  • The task “Catch 100 Aniimo” (Aniimo Master) is now linked to the current Companion Handbook instead of the first one, which ended on October 28.

Pathfinder Challenge

  • Two scripted events that sat on Little Blanket’s duel now belong to Healing Angel’s.
  • The second stage of the Rookie Island Patroller and Walking Map duels has a different title setting. Opponents, levels and rewards are unchanged.

Looks and shop

  • The 5 Creative Voyage Avatar Frames (one per region) have new art.
  • An accessory worn by an Aniimo in Home Storage can no longer be moved to another Aniimo; a message shows instead.
  • 17 hairstyle entries are marked as owned from the start.
  • The Title Paw-some Pals got a display setting, the same one the Creative Voyage Avatar Frames use.
  • Shop art for the Shadow Rose outfit was added, and the Nightborn Allure set is flagged to show among rewards.
  • One shop poster no longer says the shop is open only from 18:00 to 02:00.
  • Several shops’ item lists were re-sorted; prices and stock are the same.

Text

  • Prismana Snapshots: “Capture” is now “Photograph” in its quests and rules, and the rules say “phase” where they said “event”.
  • Butterfly Dreamscape (2), Hazy Twilight and Twilight Haze: their text says “makeup” instead of “cosmetic item”.
  • Indestructible (a Sanctum buff): “1 points of Flat Damage Reduction” is now “1 point(s)”. Its numbers are the same: 1 for every 30 P.DEF and M.DEF.
  • 3 new strings (Chompy, Dora and the order-limit message), 19 changed and 16 removed (the two removed events’ texts).

Needs verification: which hairstyles are free from the start, where Paw-some Pals now shows, and whether Voxel Coin’s limit labels show blank in English.

Still open, from the official list

  • Holo-Battle Interlink: results may be tallied early, just before a challenge is due to end, and the countdown may jump back at the start of a challenge.
  • Aniimo Dash: you may fail to turn into Thundercloud, which makes your Party disappear.
  • Astra: when it is crowded, other Pathfinders’ Aniimo may not show (listed as fixed on November 12 and still on the list).

The official list still names four more issues that the November 18 patch notes say are fixed: the Ultimate cooldown display, the Smoke Bomb, the right stick on the map and Lunara’s effects.

Unchanged

  • Every Aniimo’s stats, elements and skills, and every skill and buff value.
  • The formulas the Damage Guide rests on (3001, 3023, 3512, 3513 and the stat formulas), crit, the element chart, the duel rules, BREAK and Star-Up: re-checked against this build.
  • Holo-Battle Interlink: the same weeks, bosses and grades. The Sanctum: the same tables.
  • The map: no spawn, chest or Lumin changes. No items were added or removed, and no rarity changed.
  • Frenzied Assault (Saws and Whirlbite) still gives 0% Water Boost instead of 25%.
