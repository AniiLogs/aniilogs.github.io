Game update: November 19 patch

Game build 3989635 (v1.1.3989635)November 19, 2026

Egg HeistNighttime GatheringEventsTextEvery file changeUnchangedOn the site

At a glance

  • The game posted no notes for this patch (Steam build 26779353, November 19). Everything on this page comes from the game’s files, build 3973445 to 3989635.
  • Operation: Egg Heist: the Egg Emperor rank reward and the shop’s Collectible Blind Box, both announced on November 17, are now in the files.
  • Nighttime Gathering: 20 new item delivery quests, and 4 large ore quests that could never come up now can.
  • Events: the Whisperwake Isles Exploration Plan stays in the event list until November 24.
  • Text: 1,282 English strings reworded. A group of Pathfinders is now a Team and the Aniimo you bring are your Party, and 23 items have a new name.
  • Still the same: every Aniimo, skill and buff value, the damage formulas, Holo-Battle Interlink, the Sanctum and the map’s spawns and chests. Frenzied Assault still gives 0%.

In the game

Checked for an official note on this build: the game’s patch notes on Steam, the official Known Issues & Fixes post and the news channels of the game’s official Discord. None has anything newer than November 18, so this page rests on the game’s own files. Where the game’s text and its data disagree, we go by the data.

Text

1,282 English strings were reworded and 2 are new (the Gift Box’s name and text). None was removed.

Team and Party

  • A group of Pathfinders is a Team: Create Team, Join Team, Leave Team, Disband Team, Remove from Team, Team Channel, Team Preparation, Team Confirmation, “The team is full.” About 64 strings; they said Party.
  • The Aniimo you bring are your Party: Aniimo Party, Aniimo Party Info, Party Switch Cooldown, “Deployed Party cannot be empty”, and “party members” in skill and buff texts. About 47 strings; they said Team, Roster, teammate or ally.
  • Screens titled just “Party” and Operation: Egg Heist’s Team Mode keep their names.

The rest

  • Quest and badge-challenge dialogue, 60 quiz strings, event rules, tips and notices.
  • By language: English 1,282, Japanese 701, Korean 683, Portuguese 171, German 42, Indonesian 35, Vietnamese 13, Thai 7, Russian 2, and 1 each in Spanish, French, Simplified and Traditional Chinese.

Scenes: 2 changed

  • Whisperwake Isles: two quest scripts changed, and one character in Crescent Bay was added to one of the scene’s character lists.

Scripts: 1 of 9,805 changed
  • UIConst: the pop-up that asks you to rate the game is now full-screen with a blurred background.
Text: 2 new, 1,282 changed, 0 removed
  • New: the Gift Box’s name and text.
  • Changed: see Text above. The text files of all 13 languages changed.
Art and models: 57 files added, 3 new bundles, 8 bundles replaced
  • All of it is Pathfinder outfit data: the Shadow Rose set’s accessory and hair texture, and cloth data for 5 other outfits.
Program files: 23 modified
  • The game’s program (Aniimo.exe, GameAssembly.dll, UnityPlayer.dll), the script engine (xlua.dll), the anti-cheat module (NEP2.dll), the publisher’s and Discord’s libraries, and the version and file lists.
  • What changed inside them isn’t readable from data.

Unchanged

  • Every Aniimo’s stats, elements and skills, and every skill and buff value.
  • The damage formulas, crit, the element chart, the duel rules, BREAK and Star-Up: the formula script and every table the guides cite are the same as in the last build.
  • Holo-Battle Interlink: the same weeks, bosses and grades. The Sanctum: the same tables.
  • The map: no spawn, chest or Lumin changes apart from the one puzzle above. No rarity changed.
  • Frenzied Assault (Saws and Whirlbite) still gives 0% Water Boost instead of 25%.

On the site

  • Departure with the Wind: Collectible Blind Box now lists its shop price: 500,000 Eggshell Coin, limit 1.
  • 21 items renamed, on the item and in the recipes and chests that name it. The game renamed 23; the other 2 aren’t in the Item-log.
  • New use or description text on 134 items, and the reworded source names (Homeland Aniimo Outing, Catching new Aniimo).
  • The “New in game build” filter now shows the Gift Box.

Aniilog and Team Builder Open →

  • Team Builder: the stun that Coraliz’s skill applies is named Stunned, and 2 buff texts are reworded.
  • Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese follow where the site has the text.

  • A correction: it said 18 Nighttime Gathering quests had no order type and got one, and tied that to the official “Season Orders disappeared” fix. That was our misread of the table: those rows did not change. The three lines that said so are corrected.
