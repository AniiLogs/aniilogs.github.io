Game update: Departure with the Wind Part 2

Game build 4047363 (v1.1.4047363)November 26, 2026

New contentBalanceWorld and levelsEventsHomeland and modesItemsTextEvery file changeUnchangedOn the site

At a glance

  • This page covers the November 26 update: the game’s official update notice, checked against the game’s files (build 3989635 to 4047363), plus everything the files show that the notice doesn’t.
  • New story: Tranquil Hymn (the notice calls it “Hymn of Calm”), three chapters with the Dewlops at the Sanctum.
  • New events: Resonance Fantasia with Jaguaraun (until December 17), Annual Carnival and Starlight Echoes (November 27 to December 1), Return to Idyll for returning Pathfinders, and double collectibles in Operation: Egg Heist (until December 9).
  • Balance: Hoptail’s Tail Flick Combo, Scrubbit’s Rain-Pond Leaf Strike, Gracewing’s Help! Gracewing!, Dazmand’s Spotlight Moment and Coraliz’s Coral Spike Growth fixed; Gracewing gets a Limited-Time Reset. Jaguaraun hits harder, which the notice doesn’t mention.
  • Levels: five areas are 2 to 5 levels lower, Lv. 41–54 needs 28.8% less EXP, the Whisperwake Isles main quest opens at Lv. 50 and Wayfarer III, and 16 Pathfinder Challenges are 3 to 8 levels lower.
  • Looks: Dawn Radiance and Moonshadow Starlight in Splendorweave, paid in the new Woven Radiance, and the Renewal Pigment.
  • Homeland and modes: the big RV and RV skins at RV Lv. 16, rain waters your crops, 4 new Aniimo Dash weekly quests and a Prismana Egg in the Racing Shop.
  • Text: 2,110 new English strings, 567 reworded and 40 removed. Three skills are renamed: Blooming Drift, Baa-bble Wave and Underflow Surge.
  • Still the same: the damage formulas, the element chart, Holo-Battle Interlink’s weeks, bosses and grades, and Holo-Battle Sim. Frenzied Assault still gives 0%.

In the game

Sources: the official November 26 update notice, set against the game’s own files. Where the notice and the files disagree, we go by the files, and say what still needs checking in the game. Times are in UTC; the game’s three servers are Apac (UTC+8), Europe (UTC+0) and Americas (UTC-4).

New content

Story: Tranquil Hymn

A new story chapter, Tranquil Hymn. The notice calls it “Hymn of Calm”, which is what the quests call the song itself. The Sanctum has come back to life and the family of Dewlops born around it want to offer it the Hymn of Calm.

  • Three chapters: Song of Reunion, Song of Waiting and Song of Memory, with a reward at a third, two thirds and all of the chapter.
  • 113 quests, 92 dialogues and two new scenes.
  • Story items: Storm Song Score, Message Bottle on the Beach, Sea Shanty, Archival Chip - Della’s Song, Archival Chip - Music in the Bubble, Archival Chip - Ending 1: Offering the Song of Listening and Archival Chip - Ending 2: Singing the Answer.
  • Home progression and Title Promotion quests now have voice-over.

Splendorweave: Dawn Radiance and Moonshadow Starlight

  • Two Prismatic outfits, Dawn Radiance (its item is named Dawnlight Splendor) and Moonshadow Starlight, each with its hairstyle, outfit pieces, crown and makeup. Both come with a Change Outfit action (10 s cooldown).
  • Splendorweave uses a new currency, Woven Radiance: its shop prices are 2 to 25 Woven Radiance an item, and it also has the Swiftlight Scooter.
  • Its listing runs until January 28, 2027, 23:59 (Europe).

Renewal Pigment

  • A new item: using it gives an Aniimo one Sparkling Pigment you don’t own yet. Shadow and Dazzling Sparkling are never picked. It comes from Starlight Echoes and the Annual Carnival bundles.

Also new

  • Inventory: new tab icons and order, and an “Available” tab apart from Common Items. Items on cooldown show it, the purchase limit shows on the icon, and you can check and undo an item’s lock from the use screen.
  • Map: pick a teammate’s location on the map and teleport there. Better map zoom.
  • Profile: hide the interface and share your profile; separate counts for Aniimo with three and with six maxed attributes.
  • Appearance: “Apply Face Data” in Outfit Settings, a better camera, and Aniimo at your Home can be picked in Aniimo Accessories.
  • Network: the Function Menu shows your network status.
  • Nurture: Mark Sharing markers can no longer be placed in a Bloom’s range.
  • Research Console: prompts for other Pathfinders nearby are hidden so reports are easier to hand in.
  • Discount vouchers: the Limited-Time Appearance Discount Voucher (Annual Carnival) and the Event Discount Coupon (2,000 Lumin Crystals minimum, 500 off, for Season, Limited and Custom bundles).

Needs verification: the Limited-Time Appearance Discount Voucher’s text says 200 Lumin Crystals off a 680 minimum, but its data gives 300 off. Which one the shop takes.

Return to Idyll

  • For Pathfinders with Student I who haven’t logged in for 14 days in a row, at most once every 42 days. It starts when you log in and ends at 04:00 on the 15th day.
  • 7-Day Gift: daily login rewards, in two tiers for 14 or 30 days away.
  • Return Journey: quests give Reunion Marks (the notice: Reunion Tokens) and a Double-Cost Ticket (7 Days). Reunion Marks unlock Glimmer rewards; 1,350 give a Prismana Egg of your choice (14 to choose from).
  • Reunion Blessing: a returning Pathfinder and their team get +20% from Friends’ Companion Mode Reports, +20% from Friends’ shared Prismatic Energy and +10% catch chance in Companion Mode. It doesn’t stack.

Operation: Egg Heist

  • Double collectibles: in Team Mode at any difficulty, common and prized collectibles from chests and Umbral Aniimo drop twice as often, until December 9, 23:59 server time (15:59 UTC Apac, 23:59 UTC Europe, December 10 03:59 UTC Americas). The mode screen shows a banner for it.
  • Prismatic Iris · Dark joins Chaos mode’s collectibles, the notice says. The item was already in the files and no client drop list changed, so it’s set on the server.
  • Map rotation: in two difficulties one random map leaves the pool and a new one comes in.
  • Better pick-up and swapping with a full Inventory. You can’t use abilities while carrying an egg. Enemy HP bars show only once they’re damaged, your own only in combat. Better name display in Fair Arena.

Other events

  • An Aniimo Is Born – Co-Dev Contest (round 2): back in the files, from the update to February 15, 2027, 03:59 UTC (Europe).
  • Limited-time Home Shop stock: see Homeland.
  • Unchanged and still coming: Glamour Star ends on November 27, 03:59 UTC (Europe); Who’s That Aniimo? and Idyll Ecological Investigation run November 27, 04:00 to December 4, 03:59 UTC (Europe); Prismana Snapshots ends November 30, 03:59 UTC (Europe); the Night Parade, the Nighttime Gathering and Season 1 end December 9, 23:59 UTC.

Needs verification: the Egg Emperor 200-star pick and the weekly Collectible Blind Box from the last note, and Prismatic Iris · Dark in Chaos: all decided by the server.

Homeland and modes

Homeland

  • RV: the largest RV model and RV skins now come at RV Lv. 16 (were Lv. 21). Lv. 21 keeps its extra Home Aniimo slot.
  • Rain now waters crops at your Home by itself.
  • Furniture snaps up to floor height when there’s flooring where it lands.
  • Limited-time Home Shop stock (Resource Exchange): swap Wood Block and Mineral Sand either way, in lots of 1,000, 5,000 and 10,000, 10 times each. The files end it on December 1, 19:59 UTC on every server; the notice says December 2, 03:59 server time, which is the same moment only for Apac.

Aniimo Dash

  • 4 new Weekly Quests: give 10 likes in Group Races, use items 10 times in Group Races, finish one Solo Race, and get an A grade or better once in a Solo Race.
  • Racing Shop: a Prismana Egg for 2,000 Racing Coin, limit 1.
  • Redrock Trail solo grade times: 120 / 150 / 180 s → 90 / 120 / 150 s.
  • Group Race now records your best placement, from this update on.

Astra

  • ChillBeanz sells Iced Coffee Fizz for 100,000 Credits: it restores 300 Ultimate Points to the Team, only while exploring the Continent of Idyll freely, with a 30 s cooldown.
